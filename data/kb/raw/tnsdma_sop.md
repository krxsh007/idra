# Tamil Nadu State Disaster Management Authority (TNSDMA) Standard Operating Procedure: Chennai Floods & Cyclonic Storm Surge

**Document Reference:** TNSDMA-SOP-CHN-2024  
**Publication Date:** 2024-08-01  
**Author:** Tamil Nadu State Disaster Management Authority, Disaster Management Department  
**Applicability:** Greater Chennai Corporation (GCC), Chennai Police, GCC Emergency Operations Center  

---

## 1. Study Area Specific Vulnerabilities
The Chennai Metropolitan Area features low topography with slow natural drainage towards the Bay of Bengal through three primary river basins:
1. **Adyar River Basin:** Highly vulnerable to upstream Chembarambakkam reservoir discharge exceeding 10,000 cusecs.
2. **Cooum River Basin:** Urbanized corridor with high debris density and reduced carrying capacity in central wards.
3. **Buckingham Canal & Kosasthalaiyar Basin:** Low-gradient coastal waterway prone to tidal lock during high tide and storm surge conditions.

---

## 2. Road Network Impassability & Traffic Diversions
When water depth on roadway exceeds **30 cm (0.3 meters)**:
- Roads are declared **IMPASSABLE** for standard passenger vehicles and ambulances.
- Heavy 4x4 relief trucks and high-clearance emergency transport are permitted up to 60 cm water depth.
- Water depths exceeding **60 cm (0.6 meters)** require complete vehicular closure; access is restricted strictly to rescue boats and amphibious vehicles.

Key arterial bottleneck corridors requiring immediate early intervention:
- GST Road near Airport subway and Kathipara junction.
- Poonamallee High Road near Aminjikarai.
- Velachery Main Road near Grand Southern Trunk intersection and Madipakkam lake surplus course.

---

## 3. Hospital Priority Protection
Critical tertiary medical facilities in the core corridor:
1. **Rajiv Gandhi Government General Hospital (RGGGH):** Emergency trauma center; priority for unimpeded power grid and oxygen delivery.
2. **Government Stanley Hospital:** Key north Chennai emergency facility.
3. **Kilmauk Medical College & Hospital (KMCH):** Central Chennai facility.

Protocol for medical facility protection:
- Mandatory pre-positioning of heavy dewatering pumps (minimum 100 HP) at low-lying hospital entrances.
- If ground-floor flooding exceeds 0.2m, vertical evacuation of ICU patients to second-floor inpatient units must commence immediately.
- Dedicated fuel convoy lines must be established to guarantee uninterrupted emergency generator operation.
