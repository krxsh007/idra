# Sphere Project: Humanitarian Charter and Minimum Standards in Disaster Response

**Document Reference:** SPHERE-STD-2018  
**Publication Date:** 2018-11-06  
**Author:** The Sphere Project / Sphere Association  
**Applicability:** International & Domestic Humanitarian Responders, UN OCHA, Disaster Relief Agencies  

---

## 1. Water Supply, Sanitation and Hygiene Promotion (WASH)

### Standard 1.1: Water Quantity
All affected individuals must have equitable access to a sufficient quantity of safe water for drinking, cooking, and personal hygiene.
- **Basic Survival Water Requirement:** Absolute minimum **3 to 5 liters** per person per day (for drinking and basic cooking only).
- **Minimum Emergency Water Standard:** **15 liters per person per day** (comprising 3-5L drinking/food, 6L personal hygiene, 4L cooking/utensils).
- **Water Source Distance:** The maximum distance from any household to the nearest water distribution point must not exceed **500 meters**.
- **Water Point Queuing:** Waiting time at water points should not exceed **30 minutes**.

### Standard 1.2: Water Quality
- **E. coli / Faecal Coliforms:** 0 CFU per 100ml sample at the point of delivery.
- **Free Residual Chlorine (FRC):** Between **0.2 mg/L and 0.5 mg/L** at distribution taps.
- **Turbidity:** Less than 5 Nephelometric Turbidity Units (NTU).

## 2. Shelter, Settlement and Non-Food Items

### Standard 2.1: Covered Living Space
- **Minimum Covered Space:** A minimum of **3.5 square meters (3.5 m²)** of covered living space per person, excluding cooking and sanitation areas.
- **Ceiling Height:** Minimum internal ceiling height of **2.0 meters** to ensure adequate thermal comfort and ventilation.
- **Communal Shelter Spacing:** In mass collective centers (schools, community halls), a minimum of **4.5 to 5.5 m²** per person total floor space is recommended.

### Standard 2.2: Thermal Comfort & Bedding
- In displacement situations, each affected individual must receive 1 sleeping mat and 1 thermal blanket.
- Adequate natural cross-ventilation must be maintained to minimize airborne respiratory infections in collective shelters.

---

## 3. Food Security & Nutrition

### Standard 3.1: General Nutrition Requirements
- Average daily energy intake requirement per person across a mixed population is **2,100 kilocalories (kcal)** per day.
- Protein content should constitute **10% to 12%** of total energy intake.
- Fat content should constitute **17% to 20%** of total energy intake.

## 4. Health Action & Mass Casualty Triage

### Standard 4.1: Emergency Triage Categories (START Protocol)
During high-consequence disaster events with mass casualties, field responders must apply the Simple Triage and Rapid Treatment (START) protocol:

1. **Immediate (Red Tag - Priority 1):** Life-threatening conditions with high chance of survival if treated immediately (severe airway compromise, uncontrolled external hemorrhage, tension pneumothorax). Immediate hospital evacuation.
2. **Delayed (Yellow Tag - Priority 2):** Serious injuries requiring definitive care within 2 to 4 hours, but not in immediate danger of death (stable fractures, large lacerations without arterial bleeding, moderate burns).
3. **Minor / Walking Wounded (Green Tag - Priority 3):** Minor injuries where treatment can be delayed hours to days (minor abrasions, sprains). Directed to community first aid posts.
4. **Deceased / Expectant (Black Tag - Priority 0):** Unresponsive victims without spontaneous respiration after airway opening.
